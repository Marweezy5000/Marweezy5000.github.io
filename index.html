Building with AI

After 15 years of assessments, care plans and phone calls, I know where care coordination gets stuck. Now I'm learning where AI can actually help, and testing it by building things myself.

Where I'm focused

  • Assessments and documentation. Less time typing notes, more time with the person in front of you.
  • Care plans. Drafts that pull together history, needs and local resources for a care manager to review and adjust.
  • Outreach in English and Spanish. Clear, consistent follow-ups so members know what happens next.
